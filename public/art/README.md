# Illustration origins

The SVG animal portraits (cat, dog, generic bird and fox), neutral paw and original paw/fire-helmet mark were authored as vector paths for London Animal Rescue. `scripts/create-art.mjs` is the reproducible source. There are no stock photos or externally licensed animal images.

These original visual assets are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Illustrations are generic category companions, never evidence of appearance, species beyond the recorded category, location or outcome of a particular incident. The card labels them “Illustration”. Other species use a paw. The mark is original and is not London Fire Brigade branding.
